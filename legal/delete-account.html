Delete your World Explorer 3D account

You can delete your account in the game or on this website. You do not need the Android app installed.

  1. Open Account Center and sign in to the account you want to remove.
  2. Select Delete Account. For security, you may need to sign out and sign in again first.
  3. Read the deletion notice, type DELETE and confirm only if you want to proceed.

Deletion removes your sign-in account and associated profile, social connections, invitations, saved and owned rooms, Reality Capture photos, interiors and published exterior contributions. It cannot be undone. The account screen reports success only when its cleanup completes; if it reports a failure, contact support or retry after signing in again.

Browser-local progress and drafts are stored separately on your device. Removing the Android app or deleting your online account does not clear your browser storage. To remove that data, use your browser’s site-data controls for worldexplorer3d.io. Export anything you want to keep first.

Testing applications are also separate: use the withdrawal link in your tester email to stop future testing invitations.

If you cannot sign in or need help removing your data, email support@mail.worldexplorer3d.io from your account email address and ask for account deletion. Never send your password. We may need to verify that you own the account. Payment, security or legal records may need to be retained where required; contact support for details about your request.
