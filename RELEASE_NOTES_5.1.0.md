# World Explorer 3D 5.1.0

World Explorer 3D 5.1 turns exploration, activities, travel, creation, community
play, and companions into one connected Explorer story. The Journal, Field
Guide, and Backpack now work together across the sandbox, and regional field
play extends to every built-in Earth destination.

## Highlights

- Keep one Journal across Fieldwork, Games, Making, Travel, Community, and
  Companions.
- Explore eleven regional Field Guide packs with 180 attainable entries across
  plants, wildlife, birds, insects and arachnids, freshwater fish, and marine
  fish.
- Switch between the current regional Guide and the World Guide, search known
  entries, and follow clear regional life-list progress.
- Use a more complete Backpack with categories, item history, inspection,
  equip and use actions, quick slots, and Field Kit synchronization.
- Download a Journal backup and restore it in the browser when needed.
- Carry Explorer progress through field activities, fishing, games, travel,
  World Editor work, Blocks, multiplayer rooms, and companion care.

## Regional field play

Built-in destinations now resolve to regional slices for Baltimore and the
Chesapeake, New York and the Northeast, Chicago and the Great Lakes, South
Florida, the California urban coast, Seattle and Puget Sound, Las Vegas and the
Mojave, London–Paris and western Europe, Monaco and the Ligurian Mediterranean,
Tokyo and Kanto, and Dubai and the Arabian Gulf.

Field leads are game opportunities based on broad regional, habitat, and
seasonal context. They do not claim that a real organism is physically present
at the selected location.

## Journal and account scope

The complete Journal, Guide, companion history, and local Backpack history stay
in the current browser. Backup and restore are included. Eligible signed-in
collection receipts do not yet provide complete cross-device Journal sync.
