# World Explorer 3D 5.1.0

World Explorer 3D 5.1 brings the Explorer systems into one clearer experience
and expands the sandbox across Earth, oceans, airports, and space.

![Walking beside USS Constellation in Baltimore's Inner Harbor](assets/readme/baltimore-harbor.webp)

## Your Explorer

- Keep one Journal across Fieldwork, Games, Making, Travel, Community, and
  Companions.
- Use one Backpack with categories, item history, inspection, equip and use
  actions, and six configurable quick slots.
- Move between Field Today, the Journal, Field Guide, Backpack, Expeditions,
  specialties, life lists, companions, and seasonal surveys without creating
  separate progress records.
- Download a Journal backup and restore it in the browser.
- Raise eligible domestic animals, birds, and livestock through individual
  care, trust, and levels. Compatible companions travel aboard vehicles.

## Earth and field play

- Explore eleven regional Field Guide packs with 180 attainable plants,
  animals, birds, insects and arachnids, freshwater fish, and marine fish.
- Search the current regional Guide or the World Guide, review unidentified
  entries, and follow regional life-list progress.
- Share field leads and activity records between ordinary walking and optional
  Live GPS play.
- Fish from shore, a boat, or underwater through the same Journal and Field
  Guide records.
- Search for cities, landmarks, airports, and other familiar place names
  without requiring an exact text match.
- Follow a new First Journey that responds to the current mode and can be
  postponed, replayed, or turned off.

Field leads are game opportunities based on broad regional, habitat, and
seasonal context. They do not claim that a real organism is physically present
at the selected location.

## Roads, air, and water

- Drive vehicle families with distinct handling, a 120 MPH car ceiling,
  visible condition changes, and clearer collision response.
- Enter eligible responder vehicles and use their lights and siren after
  claiming them.
- Board aircraft at mapped airports and helipads, fly planes and helicopters,
  exit in the air, and deploy the Explorer parachute while skydiving.
- Find gates, stands, taxiing aircraft, runway activity, a control tower, and a
  ticket hall at supported mapped airports. Choose a pilot or passenger journey,
  search for a destination, or take a local sightseeing flight.
- Pilot boats and ships from mapped marinas, harbors, and ports. Nearby land
  remains visible, and large ships use a tighter camera in channels and
  harbors.
- Keep mapped ships separate from generated fleet activity. USS Constellation
  is identified as a sloop-of-war museum ship.
- Land or climb onto eligible roofs and continue walking across them.

## Equipment and city play

- Use visible Hands, field-tool, melee, ranged, and thrown-equipment actions
  from the shared Backpack and quick slots.
- Recover eligible weapons and ammunition, and exchange game items at mapped
  convenience stores.
- Meet civilians who can flee, report an incident, defend themselves, or
  respond through the bounded civic system.
- Remove expired projectiles, spent charges, unclaimed loot, downed actors, and
  disabled vehicles through bounded cleanup or recovery rules.

Ranged actions have a reticle and camera-directed projectiles, but precision
aiming and presentation remain continuing work. See [Known Issues](KNOWN_ISSUES.md).

## Space

- Fly manually or use optional Wayfinder assistance without losing manual
  control.
- Select planets, moons, small bodies, stars, and featured deep-space
  destinations, then see the active course and local guidance.
- Collide with rendered celestial bodies instead of passing through them.
- Enter accepted solid-world surfaces, traverse them, return to orbit, and
  return to Earth. Atmospheric destinations use their own journey rules.

![Wayfinder approach to Sagittarius A star](assets/readme/space-wayfinder-sagittarius.webp)

## Multiplayer and creation

- Join bounded public or private rooms with presence, chat, activities, shared
  Blocks, published overlays, and persistent room vehicles.
- Build with Blocks inside the World Editor so local and shared construction
  uses one placement and persistence path.
- Compete through game-specific leaderboards connected to supported activities
  and the Explorer profile.

## Access and performance

- Enter Earth and Live GPS play sooner on mobile while retaining the playable
  district and recovery boundary.
- Use keyboard, touch, gamepad, browser zoom, visible focus, larger text,
  increased contrast, reduced motion, and larger touch targets.
- Keep mobile entry, action, exit, and recovery usable at a 390×844 layout.
- Release superseded world, provider, scene, and temporary gameplay resources
  when changing locations or ending a session.
- Use the same airport travel and aircraft system wherever mapped airport data
  is available; density scales down on touch devices without changing the
  travel, boarding, or flight experience.

The complete Journal, Guide, companion history, and local Backpack history stay
in the current browser. Backup and restore are included. Complete cross-device
Journal sync is not yet available.
