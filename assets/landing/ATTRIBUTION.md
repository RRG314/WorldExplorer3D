# Landing Media Attribution

The files in this directory are first-party captures of World Explorer 3D:

- `hero-interface.png`: World Explorer 3D interface capture.
- `gameplay/`: Earth, Moon, Mars, space, building, and activity captures.
- `multiplayer/`: synchronized multiplayer gameplay captures.

They are covered by `MEDIA_LICENSE.md` to the extent the project owner holds
copyright in the captures. Third-party map data, scientific imagery, models,
textures, and fonts visible in them retain the terms listed in
`ATTRIBUTION.md` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
