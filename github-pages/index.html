Explore the world Release notes

One connected experience

Choose a place, then choose how to move.

World Explorer 3D combines map-informed environments with walking, driving, drone, plane, boat, rover, and space-flight modes. One globe-first workspace connects place selection, My Places, missions, multiplayer, Live Earth, and planetary travel.

Captured in the live game

A playable world at city scale.

These are direct browser-runtime captures, not concept renders. The same fixed-location world supports aerial exploration, street travel, water boundaries, bridges, terrain, and detailed urban cores.

A complete phone experience

Find it. Reach it. DeFlock it.

DeFlock Hunt runs inside the loaded Earth location with touch movement, a compact mission HUD, map markers, discovery, scoring, and virtual camera animations.

Fictional gameplay: DeFlock uses virtual representations of publicly mapped OpenStreetMap surveillance nodes. No physical equipment is accessed, controlled, damaged, or otherwise affected.

Built from real context

Global data, presented as a playable world.

Earth scenes use OpenStreetMap-derived geometry and other attributed public datasets. Live Earth separates observed aircraft, satellites, earthquakes, and water levels from modeled weather, marine guidance, predictions, and reference-only layers.
