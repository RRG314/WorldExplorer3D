One connected experience

One sandbox, from the harbor to the stars.

Walk, drive, fly, sail, fish, survey, build, and meet other players inside bounded worlds shaped by available terrain, roads, buildings, water, land cover, and places. A shared Explorer Journal, regional Field Guide, Backpack, quick slots, Expeditions, specialties, and companions carry your story across the whole game.

Built for touch as well as desktop

Carry the Explorer into the world.

Touch controls, quick slots, the Backpack, field activities, fishing, maps, and recovery are arranged for phone play without turning the game into a different mobile-only version.

One player record: Journal, Guide, Backpack, companion, and activity progress use the same game systems on desktop and mobile.

Built from real context

Global data, presented as a playable world.

Earth scenes use OpenStreetMap-derived geometry and other attributed public datasets. Live Earth separates observed aircraft, satellites, earthquakes, and water levels from modeled weather, marine guidance, predictions, and reference-only layers.
