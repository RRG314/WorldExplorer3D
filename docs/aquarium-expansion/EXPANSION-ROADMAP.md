Priority differs from the historical table below because a smaller, clearly
connected multilevel route is preferable to immediate campus proliferation.
All candidates lack approved exact interior assets and a completed license/GIS
intake. Species art can be reused only where the real exhibit's current evidence
supports the species; a downloadable model cannot establish exhibit presence.

Baltimore's next gate is sustained entry/reentry performance and remaining visual
quality, then rights-backed architectural evidence. Do not expand into additional
Baltimore wings or other venues to compensate for an incomplete first route.
Physical-phone testing, approved scans/plans and institution-specific facts
remain separate dependencies; no contacts or production publications are made.

## Baltimore sequence

| Gate | Concrete output | Completion condition |
|---|---|---|
| A — evidence and assets | Correct compound identity, entry review, public room graph, independent assets and rights | Versioned evidence, known/inferred dimensions, actual model inspection and pinned imports |
| B — playable architecture | Pavilion arrival and complete connected reef visit, finished surfaces, reliable vertical travel | Walk/run/jump, ceilings, rails, doors, elevator and exact exterior return tested |
| C — living exhibits | Shared multi-view tank, bounded animated species, water/glass and three learning actions | No clipping, wrong species, duplicate populations, dead controls or false observation awards |
| D — release quality | Room residency, offline recovery, repeated entry, Journal reload and responsive UI | Measured existing budgets; screenshots reviewed; regression and immutable package evidence |
| E — whole public visit | Remaining documented public rooms and their actual exhibit content | Each room meets A–D; no empty generic wings counted as completion |

Gate E cannot be called finished on the strength of Gate D. Unknown dimensions,
missing lawful assets or missing evidence stay named gaps. Do not fill the entire
campus with invented generic displays to make a completion percentage look better.

## Shared professional acceptance for museums

Apply the same room-evidence, finish, interaction and lifecycle standards to the
Peale visit. Its current two-gallery interpretation must remain labeled as such;
it is not a full Peale reconstruction and the Smithsonian/Met reproductions are
not the Peale collection. Aquarium improvements must not turn museums into fish
exhibits or replace their architectural identity. Expand named museums only with
their own source-supported room plans and appropriately licensed collections.
