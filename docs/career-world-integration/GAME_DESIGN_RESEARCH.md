No claim that another game’s retention outcome transfers to this app.

## Synthesis

World Explorer already combines action-based practice, career assignments, missions and personal making. The best fit is a lightweight guide over that existing identity: show one current task; preserve untracked valid progress; explain equipment and outcomes; make switching safe. Do not reproduce another game’s entire profession economy or pretend its commercial production scale is a realistic implementation estimate.

Enjoyable return reasons should be concrete: an unfinished study to resume, a new comparison worth making, a report or album to display, a route to improve, a car to maintain, a place or friend to return to. Avoid streak loss, forced daily timers and fear-of-missing-out rewards. Novelty/uncertainty and bounded practice rewards already support richer work than repeating one click.
