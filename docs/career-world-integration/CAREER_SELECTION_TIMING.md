# When to choose a career

The owner asked for a recommendation grounded in the first-player experience before the broader architecture is finalized. Default should be **after one real action/result**, while permitting an early choice and immediate free exploration.

| Timing | Benefits | Costs and mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before world entry | Strong role fantasy and coherent first kit/task | Twenty unfamiliar labels create a choice barrier. Offer a small interest-family view, real task preview, Free Explorer and “decide later.” Never force detailed attributes. |
| After universal foundation | Player understands movement, interaction, result and Journal before choosing | Universal activity must be genuinely playable at the offered location. Keep it short and optional; do not replay it for returning players. Recommended default. |
| During free play | Lowest barrier for experienced users; interests follow actual practice | New players may feel directionless. Quiet Help/Activities access and optional Continue remain visible; no constant interruptions. |

Choosing an interest should explain, in plain player language: “This suggests work and useful tools. You can explore freely and change direction later. Your progress stays with your Explorer.” It should not imply background/attributes are reallocated, an advanced qualification is earned, equipment is granted, or a mission has begun.

Starting a task is a second deliberate action after its original place/equipment requirements are shown. Selecting a focus in an existing room leaves the room unchanged. Selecting it during a long trip does not stop the trip. An unavailable local interest can remain tracked without inventing content nearby; offer a legitimate place or another real activity.

For returning players, show Continue existing work first, then optional focus. Existing character creation is not repeated. Do not infer a permanent career from old practice or auto-select the highest XP specialty. In a shared browser, local character and Journal scope must be explained before pretending a new account is a new Explorer save.

The preview separates model, onboarding and composition controls so the owner can choose timing without silently approving a different progression architecture. See options [A](ONBOARDING_OPTION_A.md), [B](ONBOARDING_OPTION_B.md), [C](ONBOARDING_OPTION_C.md).
