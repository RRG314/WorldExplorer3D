# Career families and honest content depth

Families are browsing labels over the existing catalog, not another registry, skill ladder or permission system. A course, game or minigame does not automatically become a profession. Preserve all twenty existing entry IDs and saved intent aliases; review names separately from migrations.

## Candidate extensions, not promised careers

Wilderness exploration could organize current health, equipment, terrain, weather, fishing and navigation. It needs a distinct achievable loop, evidence and failure/recovery design before becoming a card. Property development could organize current housing/furniture/commerce but is not a completed construction business. A full aviation/logistics path needs actual departure, flight, delivery, landing and return content. Mineral prospector could become an optional Geology/Mining specialization after occurrence evidence and material custody are designed. Do not add new cards solely to fill a taxonomy.

## Entry quality threshold

Each visible career should show: what the player does; where it works; a free/included starting route; original equipment needs; a meaningful persistent result; a next choice; truthful content depth and an unavailable/recovery path. Unsupported environment offerings stay disabled with a reason. Universal Free Explorer remains a deliberate choice. No beginner should have to understand all twenty labels, nine skills and multiple point systems before moving.

Suggested name review after approval: display Harbor Run as **Transport pilot / first road delivery** or **Expedition transport** until aviation work is integrated. Preserve `career:expedition-pilot` as an alias; do not rewrite historical records just to improve a label.
