## Update and removal

A source/hash/rights change requires a new reviewed import entry, conversion, output hash, asset revision and browser review. Remove or disable the registry record to prevent future selection; retain historical Journal text and canonical building identity. Never silently substitute another work under the same exhibit ID. Quarantined raw bytes are excluded from normal app startup and are not a public media package.
