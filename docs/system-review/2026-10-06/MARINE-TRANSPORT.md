- Research UI updates text and attributes every frame and stacks several opaque panels. Observation cameras synchronously read GPU pixels; ship window rendering uses another renderer/canvas. These are profiling leads, not proven causes of all stalls.
- Bridge/tunnel profiles already share render/contact heights, exact graph anchors, signed terrain bounds, portal cuts and grade limits. Grade limiting alone does not establish smooth grade changes. Preserve this existing work and audit constraint ordering and failure behavior.

## R&D and application

- [FHWA Bridge Geometry Manual](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/bridge/pubs/hif22034.pdf), §3: vertical alignment combines grades with vertical curves. Applied here as separate acceptance for slope and change of slope; a drivable height sequence is insufficient if it has abrupt crests.
- [FHWA Road Tunnel Manual](https://highways.dot.gov/media/180616): portals and cut-and-cover transitions depend on terrain and clearance. Applied here to the full road/shell envelope and approach excavation, not a decorative ring at an OSM endpoint.
- [GPU Gems, effective water simulation](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-i-natural-effects/chapter-1-effective-water-simulation-physical-models): separate geometric waves from finer normal detail and respect spatial sampling. Applied here by retaining one CPU/GPU wave authority, rather than unrelated visual and boat bobbing functions.
- [Epic buoyancy component](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/water-buoyancy-component-in-unreal-engine): distributed hull supports provide a practical game approximation. The current Earth vessel plane-fit already follows this principle; verify hull dimensions, damping and coordinate signs before replacing it.
- [Sketchfab download API](https://sketchfab.com/developers/download-api/downloading-models): downloads require an authorized account token. [Research Vessel by gogiart](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/research-vessel-300c2f04acd54ac684776a542fa45918) is a CC Attribution candidate, not yet downloaded or approved visually. Do not scrape viewer resources or pretend the procedural vessel has been replaced.
