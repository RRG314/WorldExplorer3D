# Silverstone architectural presentation

The Wing exterior replaces only the building with stable mapped identity
`overture:02a0db51-e850-4277-a81b-757736f7073a`. Its actual published footprint,
foundation and provider height ceiling are retained. A similar building name
does not qualify another building. An incompatible replacement footprint keeps
the ordinary building representation.

The original World Explorer reconstruction interprets the folded metal roof,
glazed hospitality facade, framing and 41 closed garage bays described by
[Populous](https://populous.com/showcases/silverstone-circuit),
[the BRDC opening account](https://brdc.co.uk/Opening-of-the-Silverstone-Wing/)
and [Ash & Lacy](https://www.ashandlacy.com/case-studies/the-silverstone-wing-building/).
References checked October 29, 2026. Designer and opening accounts give different
overall lengths (360 and 390 m); neither is used to stretch the mapped outline.
The provider height is an estimate, not a measured architectural roof survey.
Roof folds, bay spacing and facade details are authored interpretations.

No reference photograph, venue logo or sponsor artwork is imported as a game
texture. Existing concrete materials and static structure batching keep the
detail bounded. The current building owner publishes and retires the model;
closed-building collision, entrance identities and adjacent buildings remain
with their existing owners. This exterior does not grant public interior or
garage access and does not by itself complete the Silverstone venue pack.
