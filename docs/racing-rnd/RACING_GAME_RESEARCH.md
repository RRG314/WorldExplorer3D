# Racing design research

Evidence date: October 28, 2026. Baseline `7da846f`; current working-source improvements are distinguished from unimplemented racing. No production deployment or purchase.

Primary sources accessed October28,2026. These are design references, not permission to copy art, names or implementations.

| Reference | Public evidence | Applicable lesson |
|---|---|---|
| NFS Heat | [EA text manual](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/need-for-speed/need-for-speed-heat/pc/text-manual) | Day/night risk and reward create different reasons to drive; World Explorer should preserve optional exploration. |
| NFS Unbound | [EA Lockdown guide](https://www.ea.com/games/need-for-speed/need-for-speed-unbound/news/how-to-play-lockdown) | Temporary use, extraction and permanent acquisition are different states. Do not equate entering traffic with owning it. |
| NFS Carbon | [Developer Q&A](https://www.shacknews.com/article/43120/need-for-speed-carbon-qa) | Crews and regional rivalry can motivate revisiting places; avoid duplicating its fictional territories. |
| NFS garage/customization | [EA manual](https://eaassets-a.akamaihd.net/eahelp/manuals/need-for-speed-manual_PC_uk.pdf) | Collection management should connect acquisition, selection and tuning in one place. |
| Forza Horizon | [Official Horizon 5 overview](https://forza.net/horizon5) | Open-world exploration can coexist with driving events. Exact Car Mastery rules were not verified from a primary source and are excluded from this design. |
| Forza Motorsport | [Turn10 progression changes](https://forza.net/news/forza-motorsport-car-progression-updates) | Do not lock every useful upgrade behind repeated per-car grind. |
| Gran Turismo7 | [Garage manual](https://eu.gran-turismo.com/us/gt7/manual/home/01), [tuning](https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/gt7/manual/tuningshop/01) | Separate owned-car choice, settings, purchased parts and collection history. |
| The Crew Motorfest | [Ubisoft playlists](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/8emz7StRUkD06NncFlYFz/the-crew-motorfest-season-7-adds-two-new-free-playlists) | Themed events can mix vehicle interests without making everything a street-racing career. |
| Assetto Corsa/ACC | [Developer product description](https://assettocorsa.gg/assetto-corsa-competizione/), [physics notes](https://assettocorsa.gg/wp-content/uploads/ACCv19-physics_notes.pdf) | Tyres, suspension and electronics need genuine interacting simulation before a tuning UI can claim them. |
| BeamNG | [Physics explanation](https://www.beamng.com/game/about/physics/), [node beams](https://documentation.beamng.com/modding/vehicle/sections/beams/) | Soft-body deformation requires a structural model; arbitrary Sketchfab surfaces do not provide one. |
| Trackmania | [Replay documentation](https://doc.trackmania.com/play/watch-replays/) | Version courses and retain evidence; a displayed ghost alone does not certify an honest run. |
| Burnout | [EA manual](https://eaassets-a.akamaihd.net/eahelp/manuals/bpr-pc-en.pdf) | Accessible repair/recovery keeps vehicle play moving; spectacle should not destroy everyday usability. |

Underground and the2005 Most Wanted are historical leads, but a verified publisher/developer source for their specific progression rules was not located in this pass. No copied blacklist, proprietary scoring or unverified numerical mechanics are proposed. The researched alternatives below are deliberately independent designs.

Recommendation: geographic regional clubs with optional mastery/collector goals. Reasons are geographic continuity, variety for ordinary vehicles, and lower social moderation burden than a persistent territory economy. This is a recommendation for owner selection, not an adopted career architecture.
