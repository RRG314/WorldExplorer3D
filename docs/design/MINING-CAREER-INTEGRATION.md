# Mining engineer integration

This implements the fixed From Vein to Material row in the career delivery checklist. The existing field runtime, profile transaction/history, scene/model leases, walking collision and Backpack projection retain ownership. It adds no world excavation, economy authority, renderer, database or update loop.

## Finite jobs and admission

Three jobs: the ordinary paired-grade deposit, a narrow vein where adjacent material dilutes the batch, and a mixed batch requiring waste separation. Each ends after its conserved processing ledger, useful fabrication order and restored cell are saved; the player then explicitly chooses another job. An incomplete job resumes its original seeded formation and ledger.

Offer only an authored fictional quarry training yard. Do not offer arbitrary selected houses, parks, protected places or GPS extraction. The visible rock blocks are an authored training exhibit above supported ground; no mapped terrain is removed and no real mineral occurrence or structural safety is asserted. The curated yard admission and actual source approach must pass before this career is marked playable.

## Ownership and data

A pure, versioned seeded formation supplies the actual rendered cells and assay expectations. Copper-bearing material and dark magnetic waste are separate attributes; an anomaly alone cannot identify ore. Integer grams close every stock equation. Cell IDs and source/processing settings survive extraction, delivery, concentrate, refined material and fabrication; discarded material remains retained waste rather than disappearing.

The career state is the sole training-stock ledger, committed by the existing profile store. Local training lots never mint credits, tradable commodities, shared stock or expedition feedstock. Existing commerce and ship cargo remain separate authorities: inspection found approved ship lots and fabrication, not an Earth quarry extraction loop. The final physical order uses the conserved material in this yard; its report and bounded nontradeable product use the existing collection/Backpack projection. Shared stock would require an actual server transaction before rewards.

## Five procedure boundaries

1. Walk to all three visible faces and record their real rendered features before choosing a provisional zone.
2. Place a five-point line on the selected working face and retain geometry-bound virtual assays. Compare grades and the magnetic-waste control. Support a zone choice with the assays.
3. Mark an explicit cell volume. Reject support cells, protected edges, unsupported patterns, return-route obstruction and carrier overload before cutting.
4. Operate the physical cutter using the existing normal-frame effort recorder; its actual tool/formation contacts supply the record. Separate identified waste, load a supported carrier, then walk the retained return route to the receiving scale. Retain actual effort, positions and weighed mass. A dialog button alone cannot deliver the batch.
5. Process the delivered batch with declared recovery/refining settings, show concentrate/tailings/refining residue, allocate material to the actual yard order, stabilize the cut and restore the worked cell. Publish original inputs/outputs/loss, source and limits. Mining Survey is a projection of once-only completion.

## Bounded acceptance

First pure formation/mass/support tests; then actual mesh/contact/scale/restoration and disposal boundary. One native UI/tools/clock/IndexedDB case checks a frozen failed-save retry, reload and affected layouts. The actual source career completes each of the three jobs once on normal frames with keyboard walking, saving every accepted boundary for resumption. Journal/Profile, full backup/corrupt-ledger rollback and original-yard revisit finish the row. Accepted earlier careers and racing are reused. Failure retries begin at the affected boundary, never at an already accepted procedure. Whole-app release gates remain in the delivery checklist.
