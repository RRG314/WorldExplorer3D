## Facility evidence and limits

Science Gateway and CMS are distinct sites. The [CERN visitor reference](https://visit.cern/science-gateway) describes five spaces connected by a bridge 6 m above the road; it does not provide an operational floor plan for this game. The reception building is mapped OSM way 1062199727. Training-room distribution is interpretive and must be labelled accordingly. Avoid inventing exact unknown rooms or placing machinery across an existing road.

The CMS surface assembly hall is SX5/building 3585, mapped OSM way 97132779. [CERN's guide](https://cds.cern.ch/record/2629323/files/CMSdocumentforGuides.pdf?version=1) identifies that building; the [published civil-engineering reference](https://cms.cern/index.php/detector/civil-engineering) specifies a 53 × 27 × 24 m experimental cavern about 100 m underground. [CERN's detector overview](https://home.web.cern.ch/science/experiments/cms/) gives the detector's approximate 21 × 15 × 15 m size. Published overall scale is distinct from illustrative service/visitor circulation. The game supplies an interpretive supported route, solid machine barriers and an existing lift transfer between verified landings; no continuously moving cabin is claimed.

Mapped identity/footprint and existing access admission are required before installing content. Homes, captures, mapped indoor data and access-denied definitions retain their priority. The in-game training visit does not grant real-site access or a GPS visit. Current [CMS visit information](https://cms.cern/interact-with-cms/on-site-visits) has real-world restrictions; do not present the game as a booking/access service.
