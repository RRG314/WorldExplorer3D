# World Explorer 3D 5.5.1

Longer drives keep the same world loaded. This update fixes a road-loading stop
that could appear when travelling beyond the starting neighbourhood. Road loading
now recovers in place, keeping your location and progress.

Earth starts in location-based mode. You can still enable extended scenery in
Graphics; an existing saved choice is preserved.

## Known limitations

Extended scenery is not a complete continuous driving world. Detailed roads,
bridges, tunnels and activities still belong to the selected location. A slow or
unavailable road region can briefly hold movement while physical surfaces load.
The [existing 5.5 limitations](KNOWN_ISSUES.md) still apply.

This update preserves the 5.5 feature set. The upcoming 5.6 work is separate.
