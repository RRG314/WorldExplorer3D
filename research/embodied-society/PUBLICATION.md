# Availability and publication boundaries

The research is published on `steven/research-embodied-society` in the WorldExplorer3D repository. It retains the mapped-world application as an environment dependency. It does not update the live application, merge into `stable` or `main`, or include production deployment configuration and workflows.

Public material includes source, protocols, a technical report, attribution and reviewed evidence summaries. API credentials, private account data, raw local run archives and unrelated user records are excluded. The public history begins with a sanitized snapshot; private development ancestry is not part of the research release.

The public branch runs bounded research tests, record validation and complete-history secret scanning. Results apply to the exact commit checked, not automatically to later edits. These checks do not run a model or demonstrate survival. Live behavioral claims are supported separately by the evidence linked from RESULTS.md. Automated scans reduce exposure risk but do not constitute a guarantee that every possible sensitive fact has been identified.

Research users should cite an exact public commit, record local changes and follow the reproduction guide. Withheld raw inputs and mutable external geography/model services limit independent replay. The existing source-available license and third-party attribution remain in force; the project does not redistribute cited papers or claim endorsement by their authors.
