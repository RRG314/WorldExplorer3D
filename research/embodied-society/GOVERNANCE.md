# Research governance and progress

## Separation from the application

The public research branch is `steven/research-embodied-society`. It is not the default, production or deployment branch. No merge request, release, tag or deployment is required to publish research. The first publication uses a reviewed snapshot without private ancestor commits. The normal app remains the environment dependency; research logic, protocols and records have separate owners/directories. Research changes must not silently alter player persistence, production credentials or shared user records.

The public snapshot excludes deployment workflows and local cloud targeting files. It retains a read-only research verification workflow. Repository settings, external integrations and production state are separate operational boundaries; check them again before changing publication strategy. Never merge the entire experimental branch into production as a shortcut. A later integration should be a narrow reviewed adapter or read-only observation export.

| Public observation/visitor forks | Not implemented | Read-only export or isolated fork, privacy review and contamination controls. |

The immediate research milestone is PROTOCOL.md's single-resident acceptance series. Record any engineering change needed to pass it, then freeze a new cohort. Do not substitute more documentation for missing behavior, or a single attractive video for the run inventory.

## Change and contribution rules

Changes identify the question, affected boundary, evidence tier, resource budget and expected failure behavior. Tests must observe outcomes rather than implementation spelling alone. Preserve negative results; label debugging, exploratory and confirmatory work distinctly. Report whether evaluation is self-review or independent. Cite outside work where it informs design; do not copy papers, figures or assets without appropriate rights.

Issue reports should include a public commit, steps, sanitized outcome and expected behavior. Do not attach credentials, account screenshots, house photos or raw private run directories. For potential security vulnerabilities use the repository's existing security reporting route; do not publish exploitable details or private data in ordinary issues.

## Ethics, privacy and interpretation

No human-subject experiment, consented participant study or institutional ethics approval is claimed. Before involving visitors, contributors or personal data, define consent, data minimization, access and retention. Geographic simulations do not establish access rights to real private places. Model output and mapped content are untrusted inputs, not authority. Neither simulated needs nor conversational claims establish consciousness or suffering.

No arbitrary shell, internet tool or source modification is granted to residents. No model is permitted to change its evaluator or budget. A branch is organizational separation, not a security sandbox. Existing repository licensing and third-party attribution apply; publication does not change ownership or grant unrestricted commercial redistribution.
