Your expedition

Pick a place. Find your own reason to stay.

World Explorer is not a tour you watch from above. It is a place you enter: choose where to begin, move through it as your own character, notice what is there, and decide what matters to you.

One coherent Explorer loop

Notice something. Investigate it. Keep the story.

Nearby leads respond to the place you entered. Choose one, minimize the Journal, follow the equipped tool in the world, then record a result with a clear destination and next goal.

  • Journal is your chronology; Field Guide is what you identified.
  • Collection contains only virtual objects you actually acquired.
  • Regional goals and capability unlocks give each expedition a reason to continue.
Encounters that continue

Discover more than objects.

Some wildlife encounters can develop into trusted companions. Learn where they belong, build trust over time, then view a companion in the world or through the AR preview.

  • Habitat-aware encounters respond to the kind of place you are exploring.
  • Real reference photography supports field identification where reliable imagery is available.
  • Companions, records, and progress belong to your explorer and persist between visits.
Tools with a purpose

Carry the right equipment into the field.

A camera, detector, shovel, sampling tools, and other equipment are part of the explorer's activity—not permanent dashboard furniture. On supported activities, your character carries and uses the selected tool.

  • Desktop and mobile controls are designed around the same expedition.
  • Tools surface as the activity calls for them and can be put away again.
  • Pathfinder and Field Explorer ranks unlock additional wildlife, geology, and excavation capabilities.

Before you enter

Useful answers, plainly stated.

Is this a game?

It is an open exploration experience with game systems, but no single required storyline. Your goals can be travel, discovery, collection, creation, learning, or spending time with other explorers.

Does it use real locations?

Yes. World Explorer builds its world from geographic data and open real-world feeds, then adds playable systems and generated detail where the source data does not provide it.

Does it run on mobile?

Yes. Mobile includes touch controls and field activities. A desktop browser is currently recommended for the highest rendering quality and the largest city scenes.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for core exploration. Sign in when you want multiplayer and persistent account-linked progress.

Can I contribute?

Yes. You can submit staged place, building, interior, and photo updates for review. Contributions never overwrite the live shared world without review.
