FAQ

Does it run on mobile?

Yes. The runtime includes mobile controls, but desktop is still the best way to see the full visual range and larger city scenes.

What is Live Earth?

Live Earth is the real-time globe section inside World Explorer. It currently brings satellites, earthquakes, and weather into the same platform as the local 3D world.

Can I go inside buildings?

Supported buildings can be entered on demand. The platform uses mapped interiors when they exist and enclosed generated interiors when they do not.

Is multiplayer free?

Yes. Multiplayer is free for signed-in accounts. Donations are optional and only support continued development.

Can I contribute edits?

Yes. The Contribute/Edit flow lets you submit staged place, building, interior, and photo updates for review instead of editing the live world directly.

Where does the data come from?

World Explorer combines OpenStreetMap with open real-world feeds such as CelesTrak, USGS, and Open-Meteo, plus optional user-configured provider APIs where available.
