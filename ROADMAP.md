# World Explorer 3D Roadmap

Updated October 7, 2026.

World Explorer is growing into a connected world sandbox: choose a real place,
travel, discover, create and return with progress that matters. The priority is
to improve the systems already here, with clear controls and reliable journeys.
See the [5.5 update](RELEASE_NOTES_5.5.0.md) and
[known limitations](KNOWN_ISSUES.md) for the current scope.

## First: reliable exploration

Reduce noticeable pauses during walking, driving and travel transitions while
preserving mapped roads, buildings and regional land cover. Keep loading,
cancellation, recovery and resource cleanup predictable. Complete sustained
physical-phone review alongside desktop testing.

Bridges and tunnels need consistent carriageways, connected entrances and exits,
terrain cuts, clearances and camera behavior. Complex crossings and mountainous
or underwater approaches remain a priority for both driving and visual review.

## More convincing places

Improve recognizable landmarks, facades, storefronts, pavement, street furniture,
vegetation and people as complete scenes. Use licensed assets where they improve
quality within browser memory and rendering budgets. Avoid making every place
look like the same generated block.

Extend official GIS and land-cover coverage where sources permit it, retaining
source attribution and uncertainty. Parks, forests, wetlands and coastlines
should be recognizable in the world itself. Photo contributions should remain
attached to the correct building, with private drafts and explicit review before
public sharing.

## A richer ocean

Replace the research vessel with a licensed model that supports a coherent,
walkable deck and interior, working stations, water access and submarine recovery.
Improve swimming and diving animation, underwater visibility, vessel motion and
water presentation together.

Expand habitat-based marine life, sightings, surveys and exploration activities
beyond the current bounded scene. Whales, dolphins and deep-water wildlife need
appropriate behavior and asset budgets. Spearfishing requires its own complete
controls, equipment, feedback and progression; it is planned, not currently
available.

## Connected travel across Earth

Regional terrain and mapped buildings now follow travel in a replaceable
window, with an option to keep the original fixed surroundings. Detailed roads,
interiors and activities still belong to the starting district. Extending those
systems needs connected terrain, bridges and tunnels across region boundaries,
while preserving existing locations and saves.

Worldwide travel is only ready when long journeys remain recoverable and memory
stays bounded. A streaming toggle alone does not complete this feature.

## Space with a clearer purpose

Finish the Solis Reach art and equipment pass, improve destination landscapes,
and deepen research, fabrication and expedition consequences. Navigation should
make the next destination, return route and available actions easy to understand.
Interstellar Expeditions remain Alpha while these journeys mature.

Real observations and modeled or procedural environments should remain clearly
distinguished. Space presentation uses playable scales rather than claiming a
uniform astronomical simulation.

## One persistent Explorer

Connect character growth, the Backpack, Journal, companions, homes, vehicles and
supported creations to understandable milestones. Extend account recovery and
cross-device progress without replacing local backup and restore.

Develop shared activities, trade and player-created places through existing
ownership and permission rules. Explorer Credits remain a game currency with
no cash value. A new activity should strengthen the shared world rather than
introduce another competing inventory, wallet or progression system.

## Live Earth and contributions

Improve the discovery and reliability of available public camera views, imagery,
weather, ocean conditions and other live-context layers. Preserve provider
licensing, source labels, observation times and honest unavailable states.
Worldwide camera or video coverage is not promised.

Make exterior and interior photo editing, review and publication easier to use.
Automatic 3D reconstruction remains a development track, not the public default.

## How priorities are chosen

Fix broken journeys and preserve player progress first. Follow with measured
performance work and cohesive visual improvements, then deeper activities and
new coverage. Completion requires working controls, persistence, error recovery
and real gameplay review—not simply adding a menu or a model.

This roadmap describes direction rather than fixed delivery dates. Report
reproducible issues and suggestions through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/RRG314/WorldExplorer3D/issues).
