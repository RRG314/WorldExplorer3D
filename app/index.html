Help improve World Explorer

World Explorer uses standard Firebase Analytics to count visits and understand which parts of the game are used. You can switch to limited cookieless analytics at any time. If you are signed in, choosing standard analytics also helps count repeat play across devices. Advertising remains off, and exact GPS coordinates, room codes, messages, and other free-form text are never included.

      How the Field Guide works

      The Guide is an identification index, not an inventory. A documented entry shows what you identified, how many times you observed it, and the regions where you recorded evidence.

      Reference image
      A real identification photograph where licensed imagery is available; it is not proof that the subject exists at the exact selected point.
      Field lead
      A field lead uses broad habitat and season information. It does not report a nearby animal.
      Backpack
      A virtual specimen, find, catch trophy, or created item enters your Backpack only when you acquire it.
      Rarity
      How often an opportunity appears in the game, not real-world conservation status.
      Region progress
      Credit for new identifications and new-region evidence. Repeating the same entry in the same region may add an observation without rank credit.
      Companions
      Owned virtual companions are separate from real wildlife observations.

      About DeFlock Hunt

      Camera locations are publicly mapped community data from OpenStreetMap. Coverage can be incomplete or outdated.

      All actions are fictional and happen only inside World Explorer. “Virtual Camera Disabled” never changes physical equipment.

      Direction cones and detection range are gameplay approximations, not claims about real camera capabilities. No plates, vehicle photos, live results, or driver data are collected.

      Start Live GPS Explore?

      Your location moves the in-world walker while World Explorer remains open and visible. Locking the screen or switching apps may pause movement.

      Stay aware of traffic, terrain, private property, and local rules. Do not use the game while driving.

      Only bounded current-processing fixes and aggregate distance/quality counters stay in session memory. World Explorer does not retain a raw fix history or save a raw route.

      Field stops create personal virtual records only. They do not prove physical access, a real animal nearby, or eligibility for a competitive location reward.

      Contributor Guide
      How Editing Works
      Use presets and guided fields to contribute to World Explorer. Changes are reviewed here and are not sent to OpenStreetMap.
      World Editor Beta
      Add details to World Explorer without changing the original map source. Contributions are reviewed here and are not sent to OpenStreetMap.
      1. Select a base feature to patch, or choose a preset and draw a point, line, or polygon directly in the 3D world.
      2. Edit vertices, tags, and 3D properties like height, levels, roof shape, bridges, tunnels, and entrances.
      3. Validate the overlay, preview it locally, and save draft revisions without affecting the public world.
      4. Submit the overlay for moderation. Admin review decides whether it needs changes, is rejected, or is approved and published.
      5. Published contributions appear in the world while the original map data remains unchanged.
