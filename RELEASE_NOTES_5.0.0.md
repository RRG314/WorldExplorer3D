# World Explorer 3D 5.0.0

World Explorer 3D 5.0 connects field exploration, progression, fishing,
multiplayer building, and creator tools while preserving the bounded world and
travel systems established in 4.x.

## Overview

This release unifies systems that previously felt separate. Walking and Live
GPS now feed the same field activities and progression; shore, boat, and
underwater fishing share the same records; and persistent Blocks now live
inside the World Editor instead of a competing standalone builder. It also
includes the first regional ecology pack, expanded recurring activities, room
building and vehicle persistence, accessibility improvements, and substantial
mobile play and launch fixes.

## Highlights

- Explore on foot, by car, drone, plane, boat, submarine, rover, astronaut, or
  spacecraft with improved mobile controls and camera behavior.
- Use the unified World Editor for reviewed overlays and persistent local or
  multiplayer Blocks.
- Build a shared Backpack, Journal, Field Guide, regional life lists, and
  specialties through Field Today activities, Expeditions, and seasonal
  surveys.
- Explore a 60-taxon Baltimore ecology pack spanning plants, wildlife, birds,
  insects and arachnids, freshwater fish, and marine fish.
- Use the same field activities during normal walking and optional Live GPS
  play.
- Fish from shore, from a boat, or underwater, with catches and losses recorded
  through the same field systems.
- Create or join bounded multiplayer rooms with presence, chat, activities,
  shared building, and persistent room vehicles.
- Use expanded keyboard, touch, zoom, focus, contrast, text-size, and
  reduced-motion options.

## Player-facing fixes

- The loading image now fully covers the transition into the world.
- The globe’s selected-location marker stays attached while zooming.
- Mobile walking left and right match the direction of the thumb gesture.
- The walking camera follows behind the character during movement.
- Field and interaction prompts clear while a bottom menu is open.
- Speed displays use consistent physical units for walking and vehicles; drone
  height appears in the secondary readout rather than the speed field.

## Upgrading from 4.x

The standalone Block Builder entry has moved into the World Editor. Open
**Build → World Editor → Blocks** to continue building. Supported local and room
Blocks are retained.

Existing Backpack data is upgraded automatically on first use, with a local
recovery backup retained in case the upgrade cannot be completed.

## Scope and limitations

- The ecology pack is a Baltimore regional slice, not worldwide completeness.
- Field leads and virtual wildlife are game opportunities; they do not report
  that a real animal is present at a precise location.
- Terrain, buildings, interiors, water, and live data depend on source coverage
  and provider availability.
- Large-room capacity, worldwide ecology, promoted creature models, and broader
  physical-device performance coverage remain roadmap work.
- Live GPS is optional, foreground-only, and subject to browser permission and
  signal quality.
